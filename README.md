<summary><b>🔍 The finding I'm proudest of (click to expand)</b></summary>
<br/>

While building the trust-scoring model, I noticed **repo age alone (0.988 AUC) beat my full 15-feature model (0.976 AUC)**. A great score isn't always a great model: it meant the classifier could be quietly penalizing newer repos that are genuinely good. I flagged the bias risk and documented how to address it.
